# BLACK MARKET

**Short promotional blurb**

A premium underground-auction slot where rare watches, diamonds, gold and classified collectibles trade hands beneath warm marble, smoked glass and discreet luxury. BLACK MARKET keeps the interface deliberately minimal and lets every spin feel like another private lot crossing the block.

**Theme**

Luxury clandestine auction house. Mature, restrained and cinematic rather than cartoon-styled.

**Core game format**

5×4 slot presentation with stateless outcomes supplied by Stake Engine RGS. Production mechanics, payout table, RTP and maximum win must be locked to the final approved math package before submission.
