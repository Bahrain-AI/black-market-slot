# BLACK MARKET — Bonus Buy / Special Asset Map

The browser-ready special-feature art is stored in this folder as self-contained SVG sheets so the demo can load it without any external runtime dependency.

## Bonus Buy tiers

| Mode | Cost | Entry art | Gameplay intent |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Backroom Pass | 60× bet | `bonus-triggers.svg` / Buyer | 6 bonus spins, elevated special-symbol frequency |
| Vault Access | 100× bet | `bonus-triggers.svg` / Vault Key | 8 bonus spins, one persistent Vault modifier |
| Black Card | 200× bet | `vault-modifiers.svg` / Black Card | 10 bonus spins, boosted multiplier and two persistent modifiers |

## Special in-bonus symbols

`bonus-triggers.svg` contains eight web-ready trigger assets:

- Scatter — awards extra bonus spins
- Buyer — converts symbols to WILD
- Wild Case — spawns WILD positions
- Multiplier Chip — increases the active multiplier
- Red Phone — random win multiplier
- Counterfeit Printer — duplicates a premium symbol
- Vault Key — injects a premium symbol
- EMP — removes/rerolls low-value symbols
