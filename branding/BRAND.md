# GRIND. provider identity

**Provider:** Grind Studios  
**Consumer brand:** GRIND.  
**Tagline:** Distinct by Design.

## Visual system

- Graphite / near-black primary
- Warm ivory secondary
- Soft brass / gold metallic accent
- Minimal broken-G mark designed to remain recognizable at small sizes
- Premium, restrained, creator-led tone

## Current source assets

- `grind-logo-sheet.webp` — wordmark, mark, studio lockup and tagline sheet
- `grind-mark.webp` — standalone provider mark
- `grind-logo-animation-storyboard.webp` — six-beat logo reveal concept
- `black-market-cover.webp` — BLACK MARKET game cover direction

## Logo animation sequence

1. Metallic fragments enter frame.
2. Fragments assemble into the broken G.
3. G completes with a restrained gold light pass.
4. `GRIND` wordmark resolves from the mark.
5. The final period locks into place.
6. `DISTINCT BY DESIGN.` fades in below.

Keep the animation short, premium and quiet. Avoid casino-neon effects, heavy particle bursts or aggressive camera movement.

## Usage

Use `GRIND.` as the visible provider brand wherever the platform allows. Use `Grind Studios` only where a formal studio/provider name is required.
